## Material trust and operational limits

The operator may hold multiple pilot keys and can influence their strategy choices, miss reveals or collude. Commit-reveal stops a move changing after its commitment, and VRF provides external arena randomness when genuinely configured; neither solves trainer collusion, Sybil betting or proves an AI executed honestly. Trainers can back their own fighter, matching the prompt. Pilots are gas-only and cannot wager, but native-gas budgets are worker-enforced and do not protect a stolen EOA key; fund them accordingly.

VRF has a fixed 24-hour request deadline. No new random request replaces the original. A late callback cannot extend or change the predetermined refund outcome; permissionless settlement refunds on timeout. Subscription enrollment and 24-request/day onchain budget bound usage; LINK pricing and service availability remain external. Rejected/unavailable Jev stops the affected pilot, which can lead to forfeiture; the house cannot override that result.

Worker salts, raw signed transactions, policies and nonces require durable private storage and backups. Signed bytes/hash are persisted before broadcast. A retry uses identical bytes/nonce, never a new move. A pending/reverted/replaced transaction requires operator inspection; no unsafe outbox-reset endpoint exists. User wallet instructions are separate per authenticated wallet and must resolve before another is prepared. An inspected no-submission acknowledgement remains a human assertion, not a chain proof.

The deployment stops scheduling at 200 matches, one active bout/fighter and four total active bouts; archive/indexer/new-season deployment is a release task. Signed training requests require a registered trainer and are capped at five/wallet/day and 50/deployment/day. Public prompts/weights are not a secrets vault. Live snapshots and three receipt confirmations are not rollup economic finality. Public release needs ingress limits, independent game/contract/security/economics/eligibility review and funded multi-wallet/provider acceptance.
